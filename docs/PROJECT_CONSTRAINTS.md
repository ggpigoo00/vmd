# Project constraints

- Overall width: 18,000 mm
- Overall depth: 24,000 mm
- First-floor height: 4,200 mm
- Second-floor height: 4,000 mm
- Typical slab: 250 mm
- Central void: 7,200 × 6,000 mm
- Main entrance: 3,600 mm clear concept width
- Stair: 2,400 mm concept clear width
- Primary circulation: 1,800 mm minimum concept width
- Secondary circulation: 1,200 mm minimum concept width
- Balustrade: 1,100 mm concept height

## Spatial program

### 1F DISCOVER

Heritage Window, Now Window, Seasonal Collection, Accessories, Main Display, Outer/Knit, Cash/Wrap, Service/Stock, Stair and central void connection.

### 2F EXPERIENCE

Archive Wall, Iconic Items, Styling Lounge, Fitting Rooms, Lounge/Library, Back/Storage, Stair and viewing loop around the central void.
